DNS Filtering with Cloudflare

Overview of Cloudflare's privacy, security, and family DNS resolvers.

← Back to xCoreTX

Cloudflare's goal is to help build a better Internet—an ambitious goal indeed. Their commitment to protecting and building a better Internet for every user is reflected in their DNS service, which blocks malware and adult content. In 2018, Cloudflare announced 1.1.1.1, a privacy-first consumer DNS service. In 2020, Cloudflare announced 1.1.1.1 for Families, a security and adult content filter.

Cloudflare's core privacy resolver is 1.1.1.1 designed for speed and privacy. Their security resolver is 1.1.1.2, includes all the benefits of 1.1.1.1 while also protecting users from sites containing malware, spam, botnet command-and-control attacks, or phishing threats. Additionally, their family resolver 1.1.1.3, offers all the benefits of 1.1.1.2, with the added feature of blocking unwanted adult content from both direct site navigation and search engines locked to Safe Search mode.

For more information regarding Cloudflare's commitment to building a better Internet and delivering a safer browsing experience, checkout this article from The Cloudflare Blog. And don't forget to explore their free app that makes your Internet experience safer: 1.1.1.1.

IPv4 DNS Resolver Options

Malware Blocking Only
  • Primary DNS: 1.1.1.2
  • Secondary DNS: 1.0.0.2

Malware and Adult Content
  • Primary DNS: 1.1.1.3
  • Secondary DNS: 1.0.0.3

IPv6 DNS Resolver Options

Malware Blocking Only
  • Primary DNS: 2606:4700:4700::1112
  • Secondary DNS: 2606:4700:4700::1002

Malware and Adult Content
  • Primary DNS: 2606:4700:4700::1113
  • Secondary DNS: 2606:4700:4700::1003
